Which theory should I use?

TheoryMaterialNotes
Distortion energy (von Mises)DuctileBest match to test data for ductile metals. This is the "equivalent stress" FEA tools show by default.
Max shear stress (Tresca)DuctileSlightly conservative (up to ~15% vs von Mises). Simple to calculate by hand.
Max normal stress (Rankine)BrittleOnly safe for brittle materials when stresses have the same sign; unconservative in the mixed quadrant.
Coulomb–Mohr (brittle)BrittleUses different tensile and compressive strengths. Recommended for cast iron in the mixed quadrant.

References: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design, Ch. 5; Budynas & Nisbett. Plane stress assumed (third principal stress = 0).

Consistent Units for FEA

Most solvers have no units — they trust you to be consistent. Pick your values once and see exactly what to type in every common unit system.

Your values (everyday units)

Poisson's ratio and strain have no units — they never change.

Consistent unit systems

Rule: choose length, mass and time. Everything else follows — force = mass × length ÷ time², stress = force ÷ length².

How to fix a bad metric PRO

ProblemUsual causeFix
High skewnessSlivers at thin faces, sharp corners, tiny edgesVirtual topology / defeature small edges, face sizing, switch to patch-independent tets or sweep
Low orthogonal qualityPoor inflation layers, stretched cells at junctionsSmooth transition (growth rate 1.1–1.2), fewer inflation layers at corners, improve surface mesh first
High aspect ratioLarge element size on thin featuresAdd face/edge sizing on thin regions, use sweep or shell elements for thin walls
High Jacobian ratioMid-side nodes forced onto curved geometryRefine on curved faces, or use dropped mid-side nodes locally
Results change with meshNot converged, or a stress singularityRun a mesh convergence study; if stress keeps rising at a sharp corner it is a singularity — add a fillet or read stress away from it
